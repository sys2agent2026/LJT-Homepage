About Me
======
I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.hkust.edu.hk/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). Prior to this, I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
